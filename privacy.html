Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026.

Local Workspace is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app may access Google user data in the services the owner authorises: Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, YouTube and basic profile information.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform tasks requested by the owner. Access tokens and data are kept on the owner's own computer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties, except AI tools the owner chooses to use for their own tasks.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
